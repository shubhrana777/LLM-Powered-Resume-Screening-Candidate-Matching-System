Financial Analyst

About the role
We are looking for a financial analyst with 3+ years of experience to support
budgeting, forecasting and management reporting for a growing business unit.

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain financial models and rolling forecasts.
- Produce monthly management reporting and variance analysis.
- Automate recurring reporting using Python and SQL.
- Develop dashboards in Power BI and Tableau for business stakeholders.

Requirements
- 3+ years of experience in financial analysis or a closely related role.
- Strong Excel skills, including financial modeling.
- Working knowledge of SQL for querying the data warehouse.
- Experience with Python for data analysis and reporting automation.
- Proficiency with Power BI and Tableau.

Nice to have
- Exposure to risk analysis and investment analysis.
- A relevant degree in finance, economics or accounting.
